## Methods

The test is rolling origin expanding forecasting of organic daily successful actions (Intents-generated accounts excluded), log1p scale, with horizons of 1, 7, 14 days and a minimum training length of 60 days. [results/preregistration.json#/split/method][results/hypothesis.json#/primaryOutcome][results/estimates.json#/byHorizonCandidate/1:intents/horizon][results/estimates.json#/byHorizonCandidate/7:intents/horizon][results/estimates.json#/byHorizonCandidate/14:intents/horizon][results/preregistration.json#/split/minTrain]

Models: standardised ridge, alpha=1.0, scaler fit on training rows per origin. Candidates add lagged Intents volume, lagged price returns, or both to the baseline of usage and calendar history. [results/models.json#/estimator]

Uncertainty: a moving-block bootstrap with block length 7 and 500 resamples gives the 95% intervals; Benjamini-Hochberg control at q 0.0500 is applied across the frozen family of 9 comparisons; the last 30 days form a sealed lockbox; the random seed is 20261111. [results/preregistration.json#/thresholds/bootstrap/block][results/preregistration.json#/thresholds/bootstrap/n][results/preregistration.json#/thresholds/bootstrap/level][results/preregistration.json#/fdr/method][results/preregistration.json#/fdr/q][results/estimates.json#/fdr/m][results/preregistration.json#/split/lockboxDays][results/preregistration.json#/randomSeed]

- Models are ridge regressions on log1p-scale features; no nonlinear model class is claimed. [results/limitations.json#/3]
- source_coverage reports non-complete states: [{"dataset": "intents_swaps", "days": 1, "first_day": "2026-07-23", "last_day": "2026-07-23", "state": "partial"}] [results/limitations.json#/4]

## Long-term economic mechanisms

Interpretation, not a result: nothing in this section is computed from the frozen files, and none of it is a claim of the thesis.
- Cross-chain swaps could bring new accounts onto the network and create demand for contract calls, storage and fees; the data here do not show whether they do.
- Swap volume could mostly reflect traders moving existing balances through solvers, which would add transactions without adding new usage.
- Token price could reflect speculation and wider market mood more than product usage, which would weaken price as a gauge of usage in either direction.
- Fee-sensitive or incentive-driven flows can fade when incentives change, so a rise in volume over a short window says little about how long it lasts.
- Any mechanism would need its own preregistered test with account-level data before it could be stated as evidence.

## Reproduction

From the repository root `nearesis-app`:
- `pnpm thesis:build` rebuilds THESIS.md, the static dashboard, thesis.json and HASHES.json offline from thesis/raw and the frozen research results.
- `pnpm thesis:verify` rebuilds into a temporary directory and compares every hash with thesis/build/HASHES.json; it exits with an error if any file differs.
- `pnpm thesis:data` re-freezes thesis/raw from the local stores (the only step that needs the databases).
