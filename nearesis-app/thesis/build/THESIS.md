## Methods

Each series is a daily series. The model works on the seven-day mean of the observed days (a day with no value is skipped, never counted as zero), on a transformed scale: ln(one plus x) for counts and amounts, the logit for shares, ln(x) for ratios such as the take rate.

Five candidate models: the last value (naive); a random walk with drift estimated on the trailing 180 days; a linear trend on the trailing 180 days; a damped trend (Holt, damping 0.900, smoothing parameters fitted by one-step squared error on a fixed grid); and the median of the last three. [raw/forecast.json#/method/config/trailing][raw/forecast.json#/method/config/phi]

Rolling-origin backtest: origins every 14 days from day 150 of the series; horizons 30, 60 and 90 days; the target is the thirty-day average ending at the horizon, scored by the absolute error of its transformed value. Skill is one minus the model error divided by the naive error. The model with the lowest error at 90 days is used for each series, the simpler model on a tie. [raw/forecast.json#/method/config/originStep][raw/forecast.json#/method/config/firstOrigin][raw/forecast.json#/method/config/horizons/0][raw/forecast.json#/method/config/horizons/1][raw/forecast.json#/method/config/horizons/2]

Bands: the fifty and eighty percent half-widths are the empirical quantiles of the chosen model's absolute backtest errors at each scored horizon; beyond 90 days they widen with the square root of the horizon ratio, which is an assumption because the data cannot score longer horizons. Calibration reports how often the eighty percent band held the realised value in a time-ordered check that sets the width from earlier origins only (the verdict uses it when it has at least six test points, otherwise leaving each origin out in turn). [raw/forecast.json#/method/config/horizons/2]

Simulation: 2,000 paths from a block bootstrap (block length 14 days) of the chosen model's one-step residuals, with seeds derived from the input hash e3f4f645ee74. The crossover and the twelve-month totals are read from these paths. [raw/forecast.json#/method/config/paths][raw/forecast.json#/method/config/block][raw/forecast.json#/inputHash]

Verdict per series: FORECAST_WITH_SKILL when the model beats naive by at least 10% at 90 days and the eighty percent band is calibrated between 65% and 95%; otherwise FORECAST_LOW_SKILL. A model that cannot beat naive is reported as such and naive is used with its wider bands. [raw/forecast.json#/method/config/skillThreshold][raw/forecast.json#/method/config/horizons/2][raw/forecast.json#/method/config/calibrationRange/0][raw/forecast.json#/method/config/calibrationRange/1]

## Evidence against

- Gas is only one toll. Protocol fees, app fees and referral fees may reach NEAR-aligned parties in ways this thesis cannot see; it measures NEAR burnt as gas and the user-paid spread, and nothing else.
- A low take rate with large volume can still be the larger number in absolute terms: the twelve-month NEAR burnt by the Intents contracts cannot be computed without the fee inputs.
- The NEAR price is held fixed at the last close of 3.42 USD in every USD figure for NEAR burnt; it is not a price forecast. The price moved from 2.79 USD to 3.42 USD (22.7%) over the same months. [raw/forecast.json#/derived/lastClose/usdPerNear][raw/forecast.json#/facts/price/first][raw/forecast.json#/facts/price/last][raw/forecast.json#/facts/price/change]
- The fee window and the contract set are choices: the take rate counts NEAR burnt by the narrow set of Intents contracts over the window frozen in the fee manifest, divided by settled USD from the Explorer API; another window or a wider contract set gives another rate.
- Intents activity depends on one protocol's design choices. Private settlement since 2026-10-27 hides part of it, so lower recent values can be unobserved activity rather than less activity.

## Confounders and blind spots

- The known break on 2026-04-02: total transactions changed by -71.7% overnight (from 3,758,663 to 1,063,375) while head transactions changed by -76.7% and long-tail transactions by -0.874% and long-tail signers by -1.33%. Because the heavy head is excluded, F2 and F3 are not affected by it by construction, and the numbers confirm it. - Composition excursion in May 2026: the NEAR-token leg share of settled volume peaked at 50.6% on 2026-05-26 and averaged 10.1% in the month against 4.23% in June. - Swap data: the Explorer feed has partial days (listed in the manifest), is not all Intents activity, and shows a swap's USD value as the provider recorded it. Registry mapping decides which volume counts as stable or NEAR-chain; an unmapped leg is reported separately and never counted as either.

## Long-term economic mechanisms

- NEAR is paid when a transaction burns gas: a share of the gas fee is burnt, so the Intents contracts pay NEAR in proportion to the transactions addressed to them, not in proportion to the dollars those transactions settle. A larger settled size per transaction therefore lowers the take rate, and more transactions of the same size raise the NEAR burnt without changing the rate.
- The user-paid spread (the gap between the quoted and the executed price) is paid to market makers and the protocol's solver network; it reaches NEAR only if the receiving parties are NEAR-aligned, which this thesis cannot observe.
- Stablecoin movement between other chains pays the same gas as any other swap but carries little price risk for the user, so a rising stable-to-stable share can lower the take rate without lowering the activity.
- These mechanisms are stated as hypotheses to be checked by the checkpoints, not as findings.

## Reproduction

From the repository root `nearesis-app`:
- `pnpm thesis:data` re-freezes thesis/raw from the local databases (read-only SELECTs); add `--bigquery` to re-run the billed BigQuery cross-checks.
- `pnpm thesis:build` runs the forecast engine on the frozen data and writes raw/forecast.json, THESIS.md, FACTS.md, the static site, thesis.json and HASHES.json, offline.
- `pnpm thesis:verify` rebuilds into a temporary directory and compares every hash, including raw/forecast.json; it exits with an error if any file differs.
- `pnpm thesis:verify --dir thesis/archive/intents-vs-price-v1` verifies the archived previous version.
Input hashes are listed in the Source index; output hashes are in thesis/build/HASHES.json.
