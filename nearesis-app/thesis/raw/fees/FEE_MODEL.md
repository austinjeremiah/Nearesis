# NEAR Intents fee model: who collects a toll, and how much reaches NEAR

Read on 2026-11-14 (all "read" dates below are that day). "Official" = docs.near-intents.org, near.org, near.foundation, docs.near.org, GitHub near/intents and near/nearcore, on-chain state. Anything else is labelled NON-OFFICIAL. Page text was extracted with a fetch tool that summarises pages; quoted sentences are as returned and should be re-read on the page before they go into the thesis as verbatim quotes.

Sources that disagree or are unclear (flagged, not resolved):
1. Docs vs a search summary: one search summary said "without an API key an additional 0.2% fee"; the official fees page says 0.25% (25 bps) without a key and 0.2% is the near-intents.org front-end fee. The official pages agree with each other on 25 bps.
2. App fee split wording: the fee-config page says the integrator's fee is "split 50/50 with 1Click" with a 20 bps floor for 1Click, yet the example (fee 21 gives 11 to the integrator and 20 to 1Click) sums to 31, more than the fee set. Whether the user pays the `fee` or `fee` plus a top-up is not clear from the page. Whether 1Click's share appears as its own `appFees` entry is not documented; in raw pages the account `1csfundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near` appears as a recipient, which suggests it may.
3. Field name: the docs show `fee` in both the request and the Explorer response; one third-party summary of the newer docs used `fee_bps`. Explorer raw pages we hold use `fee` (an integer, in basis points by the 1Click doc).
4. The near.org post says protocol fees "go directly to NEAR Protocol", while the on-chain contract fee collector is an implicit account and the on-chain fee is only 1 pip. The reported 11.5% to 30.5% "protocol fee capture" therefore cannot be the 1-pip contract fee; it must come from the 1Click/distribution layer. Neither primary page defines the term or the fee base.
5. Conflicting NON-OFFICIAL figures on the fee switch (100% of fees to buybacks vs 50% vs 16.4% share) came from search results of crypto media; none is used here.

## 3. Private settlement and what stays visible

- The Explorer API `depositType`, `recipientType` and `refundType` enums include `CONFIDENTIAL_INTENTS`; that is the only privacy marker in the schema ([get-transactions](https://docs.near-intents.org/api-reference/get-transactions.md)). `amountIn`/`amountOut` read 0 while a swap is in flight, by design.
- Confidential Intents: "deposits and withdrawals cannot be tracked to each other" ([confidential swaps](https://docs.near-intents.org/integration/distribution-channels/1click-api/quickstart/confidential-swaps.md)). Launch date 2026-02-25 and general availability in July 2026 are reported only by NON-OFFICIAL crypto media in what I found.
- Official share: NEAR's Q2 report says only that "a large and growing share of near.com swap volume now runs confidential" and "confidential TVL past $30 million" (2026-07-08, [near.org](https://www.near.org/blog/the-state-of-near-q-2-2026)); no percentage. NON-OFFICIAL: Nansen reports 42% of near.com swap volume private by default (Q2 2026); that is near.com only, not all Intents volume.
- I found no official source for a "2026-10-27 change" to private settlement, and none that gives a share of total Intents volume.
- In our own data, no Explorer row has `CONFIDENTIAL_INTENTS` as deposit or recipient type (see `visibility_by_leg_type_monthly.json`), so confidential flow is absent from our swap data, not merely flagged.

## 4. Open questions that primary sources did not answer

1. Whose account is the on-chain `intents.near` `fee_collector` (`7066024d...7f05`), and where does the 1-pip fee go? No primary page names it.
2. Exact definition of "protocol fee capture" (11.5% / 17.9% / 30.5%): numerator and denominator, and whether it is a share of gross user fees or of 1Click revenue.
3. What `buybacks.multisignature.near`, `1csfundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near` and the implicit-account recipients are (names suggest roles but no official page lists them). The treasury-addresses page lists deposit treasuries, not fee recipients.
4. Whether the user pays the app `fee` alone or the `fee` plus 1Click's floor (see disagreement 2), and whether 1Click's share is an `appFees` entry.
5. Solver spread: not published; only the all-in spread can be measured.
6. The 2026-10-27 private-settlement change and any share of volume that moved private: no official statement found.
7. Exact protocol v87 mainnet activation time (only a non-official source gives 2026-11-04).
8. HSP-029 real status (page metadata says Succeeded; comments say not yet voted).
9. Whether real 1Click/Intents fee-switch buyback totals are published officially beyond "over 1M" (2026-02-24). NON-OFFICIAL forum comment (HSP-029 thread, an unverified user post): Q3 2026 gross swap fees about $14.43M, about $11.37M to solvers and distribution channels, about $3.07M net revenue tracked as buybacks, about 1,274,076 NEAR taken out of circulation from Jul to Sep 2026.
10. Quote responses we hold are dry quotes: the docs do not say whether dry quotes include the same fee tier as live quotes.
