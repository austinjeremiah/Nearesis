# NEAR fee and burn data for "The Toll Booth Problem"

Generated by `pnpm thesis:fees build` from the saved BigQuery job results. All amounts are exact decimal strings (yoctoNEAR = 10^-24 NEAR). Window 2025-11-10 to 2026-11-13.

## Definitions

- **Fee burnt** = `tokens_burnt` of BigQuery `execution_outcomes` rows (gross, per outcome). Burnt vs paid-to-contract: not verified here. nearcore documents a 30% reward to the called contract up to protocol version 86 and 0% from 87 (docs/economics/SEMANTICS.md); the version 87 mainnet activation date is unverified, so no split is applied and all figures are gross `tokens_burnt`.
- **Narrow set** (core Intents contracts): intents.near. **Wide set** (core + settlement footprint): bridge-refuel.hot.tg, intents.near.
- **Difference narrow vs wide**: the wide set adds bridge-refuel.hot.tg; those are bridge / token contracts that execute receipts as part of Intents settlement and that get at least 50% of their own burn from Intents-origin receipts. Wide counts all burn on those accounts, not only Intents calls.
- **take_rate_gas** = (NEAR burnt by Intents contracts x day close) / (successful known-USD Intents volume) in basis points. It is the gas/burn toll only.

## What is and is not captured

- Captured: gross tokens burnt and gas of every execution outcome (369 days of 369); transaction conversion burn for 369 days.
- Not captured: the conversion burn is NOT in the executor series (it is charged at the transaction outcome, executor = signer); see tx_fee_intents_daily. Private/shielded Intents settlement after 2026-10-27 is not observable. Receipts of Intents transactions that execute on other contracts are only in the wide set when the account passed the footprint rule. Wide accounts below the day's top 500 executors are not in the wide sum (counted in wide_accounts_without_row).
- tokens_burnt is FLOAT64 at the source; values above 2^53 yocto are already rounded there.
- Missing days (not fetched): outcomes 0, transactions 0.
